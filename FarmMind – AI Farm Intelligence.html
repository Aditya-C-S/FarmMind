Project Overview

A unified farm decision framework combining disease detection, weather-driven prediction and economic loss quantification for Rice and Tomato cultivation, turning a leaf photo into a rupee-value decision.

🍅 Tomato – TYLCV

Yellow Leaf Curl Virus, whitefly-transmitted, 20–100% yield loss.

🌾 Rice – Sheath Blight

Rhizoctonia solani, fungal, 10–50% yield loss.

Key Features

  • Real-time leaf disease detection (YOLO11s)
  • Pre-symptom occurrence forecast from live weather
  • 5-day disease progression (AUDPC)
  • Economic loss estimate in ₹ for your farm
  • ICAR-sourced treatment recommendations
  • Live Agmarknet market prices
